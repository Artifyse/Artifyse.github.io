What is Pear?

Pear is a Tech company from Singapore, known for its smart devices such as the Uphone, Upad and PearWatch, founded by Darryl and his team in 2026

What Pear strives for

At Pear, we strive to reduce tech waste using sustainable materials and provide our customers with the best service we re able to provide.

Why choose Pear?

Pear uses many recycled and sustainable materials in its devices and gets materials from ethical sources


Our Values

Privacy

We believe that privacy should be a basic human right, which is why we will never collect data from our users' devices without their consent



Education

As digital skills are getting more important in the world, we are working with many educational institutes across many different countries to enchance learning in and out of the classroom

Innovation

Whether its the materials, batteries, or size, we strive to find and innovate new ways to improve our devices with sustainability, longevity, and everyone in mind


Environment

As natural resources are being depleted, we develop our devices with the planet's environment in mind, which is why our devices are made to last, with recycled materials used as components
